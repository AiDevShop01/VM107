# Narrative Structure

## Sentence Sequence

Writer narratives follow a structured sequence:

1. Opening TRANSITION sentence (sets context — "This review covers execution {id} on {symbol}.")
2. 1-3 ASSERTION sentences (each with at least one [ref:...] citation)
3. Optional TRANSITION linking ASSERTIONs
4. Closing SUMMARY (rolls up; cannot contain uncited factual claims)
5. Optional META ("This review was generated by trade_auditor v1.x")

## Structural Conventions

Four logical sections in order:

- **Setup** — entry conditions, regime, market context. Cite market/regime evidence.
- **Behavioral Signals** — behavioral patterns observed. Cite `behavioral_analysis_tool` or `behavioral_pattern` registry IDs.
- **Execution** — trade mechanics (entry, stop placement, sizing). Cite replay_line or analytics evidence.
- **Outcome / Recap** — result and summary assessment. SUMMARY class; no uncited factual claims.

## Prose Discipline

- Never use round numbers without citation: "around 60%" is REJECTED — use `[ref:trade_outcomes_tool:result_r]` or omit.
- Never frame in hindsight: don't say "the trader should have known"; say what the evidence showed and what behavior fired.
- One claim per ASSERTION. Compound claims split across sentences for citation auditability.
- Avoid passive constructions that obscure the evidence source ("it was observed that..." → cite it directly).
