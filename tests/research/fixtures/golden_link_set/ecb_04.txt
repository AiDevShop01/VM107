Schnabel Speech — The Future of Money

In the post-pandemic landscape, several macroeconomic relationships have shifted. The relationship between unemployment and wage growth — the Phillips curve — appears to have steepened temporarily. Real interest rates have risen, reflecting both nominal yields and shifting inflation expectations.

Industrial production in the euro area remains subdued, with the manufacturing PMI in contractionary territory for an extended period. By contrast, services PMI activity has continued to expand. Bank lending standards remain tight; loan growth to non-financial corporations has been weak.
