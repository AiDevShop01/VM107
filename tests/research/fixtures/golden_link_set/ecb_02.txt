Lagarde Speech — ECB Forum on Central Banking, Sintra 2026

The disinflationary process has been broad-based. Core HICP inflation, excluding energy and food, fell to 2.6 percent in the latest reading. Services inflation has been stickier, reflecting the strong labour market and continued wage growth in the euro area. The unemployment rate has remained at its historic low of 6.4 percent.

Long-term inflation expectations remain anchored around 2 percent. The Governing Council will continue to follow a data-dependent and meeting-by-meeting approach to determining the appropriate level and duration of restriction. The euro has been broadly stable against the U.S. dollar over the past quarter.
