FOMC Minutes — September 2025

Participants noted that wage growth, as measured by average hourly earnings and the Employment Cost Index, has moderated meaningfully from its 2022 peak. Real consumer spending continued to advance, and retail sales rose at a moderate pace. Housing market activity, including housing starts and existing home sales, remained subdued amid elevated mortgage rates.

The 10-year Treasury yield rose during the inter-meeting period, reflecting both higher near-term policy expectations and a higher term premium. Participants generally judged that policy was sufficiently restrictive to bring inflation back to 2 percent over time. Consumer sentiment has improved gradually but remains below pre-pandemic levels.
