NBER Working Paper 32401 — Labor Market Tightness, Job Vacancies, and Wage Setting

We construct a high-frequency measure of labor market tightness using vacancy-to-unemployment ratios across U.S. metropolitan areas. Tight labor markets in 2021-2023 were associated with elevated wage growth, particularly for low-wage workers. The aggregate nonfarm payrolls series and the unemployment rate are useful but coarse summaries of these underlying dynamics.

We estimate the implications for the natural rate of unemployment (u-star) and find evidence that u-star may have risen modestly. The labor market remains tight relative to historical norms, supporting wage growth and core services inflation. Productivity dynamics are also discussed.
