Powell Press Conference — Opening Remarks, April 2026

Good afternoon, everyone. My colleagues and I remain squarely focused on our dual mandate to promote maximum employment and stable prices for the American people. Today, the FOMC decided to leave our policy interest rate unchanged and to continue reducing our securities holdings.

Inflation has come down significantly from its mid-2022 peak, but core inflation remains too high. The 12-month change in the consumer price index was 3.5 percent in the latest report, down from a peak of over 9 percent. The labor market remains strong; nonfarm payroll employment has continued to expand at a solid pace, and unemployment is near a 50-year low at 3.8 percent. Industrial production has been roughly flat over the past six months.
