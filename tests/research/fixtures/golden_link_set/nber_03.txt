NBER Working Paper 32201 — Real Yields, Term Premium, and the Convenience Yield on Treasuries

We decompose nominal 10-year Treasury yields into a real yield component, expected inflation, term premium, and convenience yield. The convenience yield on Treasury collateral has compressed and rebuilt several times over 2022-2025, with implications for the broader transmission of monetary policy.

The term premium has risen substantially from its 2020 lows. We examine the relationship between term premium estimates and the U.S. dollar index, finding a robust positive co-movement during regime shifts. Our findings have implications for r-star and the calibration of long-term policy stance.
