ECB Press Release — Monetary policy decisions, June 2026

The Governing Council today decided to lower the three key ECB interest rates by 25 basis points. Inflation has come down significantly. Based on the Governing Council's updated assessment of the inflation outlook, headline HICP inflation is projected to average 2.5 percent in 2026 and 2.2 percent in 2027, converging toward the 2 percent medium-term target.

The unemployment rate in the euro area has remained close to its historical low, supporting still-firm wage growth. Real GDP growth in the euro area was modest in the first quarter. The pass-through of past interest rate hikes to credit conditions and lending volumes continues to weigh on demand.
