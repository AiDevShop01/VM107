ECB Press Release — Monetary policy decisions, March 2026

The Governing Council today decided to keep its three key interest rates unchanged. The Governing Council has made good progress in bringing inflation back to its 2 percent medium-term target. According to the new staff projections, HICP inflation is projected to be 2.3 percent in 2026 and 2.0 percent in 2027.

The unemployment rate in the euro area is forecast to remain near record lows, while real GDP growth is expected to pick up gradually as private consumption and external demand strengthen. Wage growth, while still elevated, is expected to moderate as inflation declines and labour market tightness eases.
