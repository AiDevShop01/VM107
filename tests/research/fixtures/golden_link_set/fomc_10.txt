Beige Book — Summary of Commentary on Current Economic Conditions

Overall economic activity expanded slightly across most Federal Reserve Districts. Consumer spending was mixed; retail sales softened modestly while services activity continued to expand. Manufacturing output ticked down in several Districts, consistent with the broader manufacturing PMI weakness. Industrial production was roughly flat on net.

Labor market conditions remained tight in most Districts, though contacts reported some easing in worker availability. Wage growth was moderate. Most contacts expected slight-to-modest growth in coming months but flagged downside risks from elevated interest rates and weaker global demand. Housing starts declined modestly.
