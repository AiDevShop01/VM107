ECB Economic Bulletin — Issue 3, 2026

Headline HICP inflation in the euro area was 2.4 percent in March, down from 2.6 percent in February. Core HICP inflation — excluding energy and food — eased to 2.9 percent. Services inflation, at 4.0 percent, continues to be the main driver of underlying inflation. The unemployment rate stands at 6.5 percent, near historic lows.

The euro area economy expanded by 0.3 percent quarter-on-quarter in the first quarter of 2026, supported by a modest pickup in private consumption and services activity. The energy price shock has continued to fade. Consumer confidence has improved gradually but remains below its long-run average. Retail sales rose modestly.
