Lagarde Press Conference — Q&A on Policy Stance, May 2026

Question: How does the Council view the path of yields, and what role does the euro exchange rate play?

President Lagarde: Long-term yields across the euro area have moved largely in line with global developments. Sovereign spreads have remained contained, reflecting the credibility of our policy framework. The euro exchange rate is not a policy target, but it is one factor we monitor for its effect on inflation through import prices. The Governing Council remains committed to bringing inflation sustainably back to 2 percent over the medium term. Industrial production in the bloc remains weak but consumer sentiment has improved.
