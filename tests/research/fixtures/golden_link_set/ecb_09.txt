ECB Press Release — Monetary policy decisions, January 2026

The Governing Council today decided to keep the three key ECB interest rates unchanged. The incoming data have broadly confirmed the Governing Council's previous assessment of the medium-term inflation outlook. HICP inflation declined to 2.9 percent in December, while underlying inflation indicators eased further.

The euro area labour market continues to demonstrate resilience, with the unemployment rate at 6.4 percent. Wage growth, although elevated, is expected to moderate over the course of 2026. Real GDP growth is expected to remain subdued in the near term, supported gradually by recovering household real incomes.
