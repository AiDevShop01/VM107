Lane Speech — Inflation Dynamics in the Euro Area

The disinflation process is well advanced. Headline HICP inflation has fallen sharply from its 10.6 percent peak in October 2022. Core HICP inflation has been more persistent, reflecting the lagged pass-through of past energy shocks into goods and services prices. The Beveridge curve in the euro area continues to point to a tight, but easing, labour market.

The neutral rate of interest — the level at which monetary policy is neither restrictive nor accommodative — remains a key concept for calibrating policy. Recent estimates suggest the natural rate may have edged higher, though substantial uncertainty surrounds these estimates.
