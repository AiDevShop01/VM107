FOMC Minutes — May 2026

Participants observed that inflation as measured by the price index for personal consumption expenditures (PCE) had eased over the past year but remained elevated. Core PCE inflation, which excludes the volatile food and energy components, continued to show progress toward the Committee's 2 percent objective. Several participants noted that the labor market remained tight, with payroll employment rising by 175,000 per month on average over the prior three months and the unemployment rate holding near historic lows.

Participants discussed the path of long-term Treasury yields, with the 10-year yield trading in a range near 4.4 percent. Most participants judged that maintaining the current target range for the federal funds rate at this meeting was appropriate.
