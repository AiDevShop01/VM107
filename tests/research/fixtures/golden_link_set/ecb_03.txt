ECB Press Release — Monetary policy decisions, April 2026

The Governing Council today decided to keep the three key ECB interest rates unchanged. Inflation has continued to decline. According to Eurostat's flash estimate, annual inflation was 2.4 percent in March. Most measures of underlying inflation have eased further. Wage growth is gradually moderating.

Past interest rate increases continue to be transmitted forcefully to financing conditions. Tight financing conditions are dampening demand, and this is helping to push down inflation. The Governing Council is determined to ensure that inflation returns to its 2 percent medium-term target in a timely manner.
