FOMC Statement — June 2026

The Committee judges that the recent moderation in inflation has been welcome, though core CPI inflation remains above the 2 percent objective. The unemployment rate has edged up to 4.1 percent while nonfarm payrolls continued to expand at a solid pace. Real GDP growth in the first half of 2026 has been moderate.

In support of its goals, the Committee decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 5.25 to 5.50 percent. The Committee will carefully assess incoming data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks. Recent indicators suggest that economic activity has continued to expand at a solid pace, although job gains have moderated and the unemployment rate has moved up but remains low.
