de Guindos Speech — Financial Stability and the Euro Area Banking Sector

Euro area banks have remained resilient throughout the recent monetary tightening cycle. Loan growth has slowed and the credit impulse has turned negative, reflecting both tighter standards and weaker demand. Non-performing loan ratios remain low but have ticked up modestly in commercial real estate exposures.

The euro has traded in a narrow range against the U.S. dollar in recent weeks. Government bond yields across the euro area have moved roughly in line with U.S. Treasury yields, though sovereign spreads have remained contained. The German 10-year Bund yield is currently near 2.5 percent.
